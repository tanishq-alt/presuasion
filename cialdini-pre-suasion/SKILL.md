## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### The Central Thesis
**Pre-suasion** = the process of arranging for recipients to be receptive to a message *before* they encounter it. The moment just before the ask is a **privileged moment** — a time-limited window of unusual receptivity. "To persuade optimally, it's necessary to pre-suade optimally." The question stops being *what* to say and becomes *what to say just before what you want to say.*

### The Two Mechanisms of Pre-Suasion
1. **Channeled attention produces two downstream distortions in favor of the focal concept:**
   - **"The Importance of Attention Is Importance"** (ch3) — Kahneman: *"Nothing in life is as important as you think it is while you are thinking about it."* Whatever you place at the center of attention gets over-weighted.
   - **"What's Focal Is Causal"** (ch4) — whatever's salient is also presumed to be the cause. Camera angles, celebrities, visible leaders — all overweighted.
2. **Elements fire when readied, not just when ready** (ch9). Once a concept gets attention, linked concepts become more accessible and unlinked ones get *suppressed*. Pre-fetched hyperlinks launched by one click.

### Three Commanders of Attention Not Requiring Effort
- **Attractors** (ch5): the **sexual**, the **threatening**, the **different** (orienting reflex). Each is conditional:
  - Sex sells only sex-adjacent categories.
  - Fear works only when paired with a specific actionable step; otherwise triggers denial.
  - Difference works via cuts (video) or novelty (static media) or decoy-by-contrast — but only if the spotlighted item has worth.
- **Magnetizers** (ch6): the **self-relevant**, the **unfinished** (Zeigarnik), the **mysterious.** Mysteries recruit *explanation*, which is why they beat descriptions and questions for retention and understanding.

### The Mechanism Beneath Attention: Associations (ch7)
- Language's main job is not description — it's **influence**, by routing attention to associatively pre-loaded regions.
- **Word choice is association choice.** SSM Health's "no bullet points / no attack / no beat the competition" policy. Stanford's crime-metaphor one-word swap (beast vs virus) produced a **22% policy shift** — more than gender (9%) or party (8%).
- **Metaphor transfers both meaning and implied solution.**
- **Implicit egoism:** anything connected to the self gets overvalued. Same birthday / initials / name → doubling of agreement rates. Coca-Cola's "Share a Coke" first-name campaign produced Coke's first US sales increase in a decade.
- **Fluency is a truth signal.** Rhyme is judged more true. Hard-to-pronounce names stay lower in law-firm hierarchies.

### Geographies of Persuasion (ch8)
- **External geography:** where you work determines what you think. Cialdini wrote his first popular book from a home desk, not a university one, because the home's cue-load matched the audience. Consultants printed photos of client employees to carry the cue-load into closed conference rooms.
- **Internal geography:** the "units of experience" in you — cough, nausea, confidence, calm — are activated by directed attention. Medical-student syndrome; happiness skills of the elderly.
- **For moving others under stereotype threat:** eliminate triggers (don't ask women to record gender before a math test), add counter-cues (female math teachers monitor the exam), use self-affirmation ("write about a personal value") — produced +1 letter grade in women's physics performance.

### The Seven Universal Principles (ch10, 11, 12)
| Principle | Core ask | Pre-suasive sharpener |
|---|---|---|
| **Reciprocation** | "People say yes to those they owe" | Give first — **meaningful + unexpected + customized** |
| **Liking** | "People say yes to those they like" (correction: *to those who feel they like them*) | Similarities + genuine compliments |
| **Social Proof** | "Similar others do this" | Twin levers: **validity** + **feasibility** |
| **Authority** | "The messenger is the message" | **Credible authority** = expertise + trustworthiness; mention a weakness *first* to install trust |
| **Scarcity** | "We want what we can have less of" | Frame as loss (loss > gain); purchase limits more than double sales |
| **Consistency** | "We align with our prior commitments" | Small public first step → larger congruent later step; reminders launch prefetched commitments |
| **Unity** (seventh) | "Of us, not like us" — shared identity | Kinship, place (home/locality/region), **synchrony** (music, matched movement), **co-creation**, **asking for advice** |

### Neidert's Stage-Principle Match
| Stage | Goal | Principles |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Cultivating positive association | Favorability to you | Reciprocation + Liking |
| 2 — Reducing uncertainty | Decision feels wise | Social Proof + Authority |
| 3 — Motivating action | Actual yes | Consistency + Scarcity |
| (Throughout: Unity for durable bond.)

### Making Change Durable (ch14)
- **Commitment anchor.** An active, effortful, voluntary step taken during the pre-suasive moment locks the preference into self-perception. The patient who writes in the appointment card drops no-shows 18%.
- **Daily cue environment.** Furnish recurring spaces with cues that automatically route attention to chosen preferences. One sign — "Hand hygiene protects *patients* from catching diseases" — produced +45% soap use when "protects *you*" produced zero effect.
- **If/when-then plans.** "When [specific cue], then [specific action]." Install the daily reminder yourself. Epilepsy medication adherence: 55% → 79%; opiate addicts completing a job résumé: 0% → 80%.

### Operator's Correctives
- **Mere reminders** usually neutralize bias. Sunny-day self-rating inflation disappears after the interviewer asks "how's the weather there?"
- **Deliberation** is the slower corrective — but defeated by rush, fatigue, overload. In modern life, deliberation rarely arrives.

### Ethical Framing (ch13)
- Reputational argument alone is insufficient; half of executives expect not to get caught.
- **Three internal tumors of an unethical culture:** poor employee performance (–20% on business-reasoning tasks), high turnover (80% quit vs 51%), and employee fraud (77% more cheating from those who chose to stay in the dishonest unit).
- Pre-suasion works best on **true** cases. Applied to false ones, the tumors compound faster than the gain.

### One-Sentence Summary
> **"Who we are with respect to any choice is where we are, attentionally, in the moment before the choice."**

## Scope & Limits

This skill covers the science and practice of pre-suasion — what to do just *before* an appeal. It complements the six principles of influence from Cialdini's earlier *Influence*. For the tactical *during-the-conversation* phrases to use once the pre-suasive frame is set, see the companion `jones-exactly-what-to-say` skill. For post-conversation retention/habit work, the commitments and daily-cue framework in ch14 is the operator's tool. The frameworks apply to persuasion ethically applied to *true* cases; applied to false ones, the ethics chapter (ch13) documents the self-inflicted costs.
